How I work

Most strategic advice begins with a framework. I begin with the market.

Every engagement combines structured research, AI-assisted analysis, adversarial testing and verification to identify the mechanisms that actually drive change in your competitive environment.

The process draws evidence from multiple sources:

  • Market structure and economics
  • Competitive position and incentives
  • Customer behaviour and purchasing dynamics
  • AI capability development
  • Adversarial testing of assumptions

The objective is not a long list of options. The objective is a decision.
